# Company Management Desk — V1.2 Beta

The platform owner uses three destinations: **Dashboard**, **Companies** and
**System**. Company Admins continue to manage their own projects and teams.

- **Dashboard:** saved company, user, project and site/task totals, pending
  applications and recent administrative changes.
- **Companies:** search saved companies or applications, inspect the next action,
  and select Company → Project → Site/task → Submission history. Breadcrumbs keep
  the selected record in context. Submission history is administrative metadata;
  this view does not grant engineering approval or evidence access.
- **System:** existing account, invitation, industry, audit, maintenance, support
  and settings tools. Existing protected actions retain their confirmations.

## Onboard a company

1. Select **New company** and enter the company name, Admin email and industry.
2. Record the application, then review it. Rejection requires a reason.
3. Approve the application and separately provision the company.
4. Separately invite its Admin. An invitation is bound to its company and
   industry; the Admin creates projects after accepting it.

An issued invitation, an accepted invitation and a delivered email are different
states. The private invitation link is displayed only when first created. Follow
the existing invitation controls to replace an expired, revoked or lost link.

The wizard also opens as a normal page when scripting is unavailable. Validation
and failed saves retain entered values. Close the wizard to return to the saved
records table; the parent search and navigation context are retained.
