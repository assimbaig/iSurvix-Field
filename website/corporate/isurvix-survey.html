FOR FIELD TEAMS AND COMPANIES

One path from site work to review.

For field engineers

Open your assigned task, update site information and upload evidence by category. Civil, Electrical and Tower photos stay organised for submission, without choosing checklist points in the field.

For reviewers

Find checklist points and their linked photos in one workspace. QA can correct evidence links, record observations and return the task when the engineer needs to provide more information.

For companies

Manage invitations, company and project access, and the people assigned to the work. Review decisions and submission revisions remain traceable as tasks move through the project workflow.

  1. EngineerUpload and submit
  2. QACheck evidence and review
  3. Team LeadReview the submission
  4. ClientReview when required

The client step follows your project configuration. AI-assisted suggestions, when available, support evidence review. Human decisions remain required; a photo link does not approve the work.

Getting started with your company
  1. Receive an invitation from your administrator.
  2. Check the company, project and task assigned to your account.
  3. Keep site information and evidence together, then submit for review.
  4. Follow the recorded feedback if the task is returned for correction.
