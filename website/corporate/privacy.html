PUBLIC WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy information.

This page explains the public website features reviewed on 6 November 2026. It does not replace a company-specific agreement for the iField application.

Enquiries you choose to send

The enquiry builder prepares text for your email application, or lets you copy it. Completing the builder does not itself send an enquiry to iSurvix. Review the recipient and content before sending. Your email application and provider handle the draft and transmission.

If you email info@isurvix.com, the information you send is available to the team handling your enquiry. Include only information needed for the discussion. Do not send passwords, access keys or sensitive site records through the public enquiry form.

Website requests and external resources

Our hosting service receives technical information when your browser requests a page or file. This can include the IP address, requested URL, browser information and request time. Browser protection and caching can affect how pages load.

Interactive maps, when opened, request map resources from external providers. Product links may take you to another website with its own information-handling arrangements. These services receive the requests your browser makes to them.

iField accounts and project information

The homepage Login menu opens the separate iField application at app.isurvix.com. Account access, company membership and project permissions are managed in that application. This public website does not request your iField password or display private project evidence.

For questions about company records, project access or retention, contact your company administrator and iSurvix. Applicable arrangements depend on the agreed service and your company's configuration.

Questions or corrections

Email info@isurvix.com with your question about this page or the information you supplied. Describe the request without including credentials. We will review it in the context of the service concerned.

This page makes no certification or statutory-compliance claim. Service-specific notices and agreements should be confirmed before sharing confidential project material.
