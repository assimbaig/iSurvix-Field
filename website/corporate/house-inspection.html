HOUSE CONSTRUCTION INSPECTION

Build with evidence.
Review at every stage.

Residential inspections at agreed construction stages, with observations, actions and closure records.

FROM FOUNDATION TO HANDOVER
A STAGE-BASED INSPECTION SCOPE

Check before the work
gets covered.

Inspection scope and attendance are agreed around the drawings, site programme and required hold points.

01

Ground & foundations

Review excavation, founding-level observations, foundation preparation and available geotechnical records.

02

Structure & pre-pour

Check reinforcement, cover, formwork, embeds and drawing alignment before concrete placement.

03

Concrete quality

Review placement and curing observations alongside available slump, cube or cylinder test records.

04

Masonry & waterproofing

Record masonry, wet-area and roof-waterproofing observations at agreed inspection stages.

05

MEP & finishes

Coordinate visible service interfaces before concealment and record finishing defects against the agreed scope.

06

Snagging & handover

Prepare a photographic snag register, record assigned actions and document reinspections.

THE INSPECTION WORKFLOW

Plan. Inspect.
Record. Close.

01

Agree hold points

Define drawings, inspection scope, access and the stages requiring attendance.

02

Inspect the stage

Record site observations and photographs against the agreed checklist.

03

Assign the actions

Issue clear observations, responsible parties and target closure dates.

04

Verify closure

Review corrective evidence and arrange reinspection where required.
