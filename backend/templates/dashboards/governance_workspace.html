CLIENT REVIEW

Review before deciding

01 · Inspect the deliverable
Open the final checklist and review its source evidence.

02 · Record your decision
Choose Approved or Rejected and add your decision reference or comments.

03 · Keep an accountable record
Approval closes the routed activity. Rejection keeps it open and preserves the engineering approval locks. Your identity is recorded with the decision.

03 · Record your quality decision
