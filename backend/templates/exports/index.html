Each workbook has Data, Columns and Export sheets. Its record includes prepared date/time, section, row count, source fingerprint and schema version. Excel download is restricted to you and rechecks current access and source changes. Changed records require a fresh export.

Maximum 50,000 rows and 30 MiB per file; two active requests and 20 requests per rolling 24 hours. Download files expire after 48 hours; the export record stays in your history. Queued work waits safely if the export worker is offline. A retry is limited to three attempts.
